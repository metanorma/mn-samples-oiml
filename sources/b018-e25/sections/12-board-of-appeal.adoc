== Board of Appeal

The Board of Appeal is independent of the Management Committee.

=== Composition

==== {blank}

. a Chairperson appointed by the CIML; and

. four members appointed by the CIML.

=== Tasks

The tasks of the Board of Appeal are to

. manage appeals against decisions of the Management Committee relating to

i. participation in the OIML-CS, and

ii. Legal Metrology Experts and Management System Experts,

. recommend solutions to any other dispute referred to it with regard to the application of the rules of the OIML-CS.
