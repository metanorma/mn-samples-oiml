== Responsibilities of the CIML within the OIML-CS

The CIML is responsible for

a) appointing the Chairperson of the Management Committee,

b) appointing the Deputy Chairperson of the Management Committee,

c) appointing the Chairperson and members of the Board of Appeal,

d) approving revisions to B 18, and

e) approving proposals of the Management Committee concerning

i. including a new category of measuring instrument in the OIML-CS,

ii. moving a category of measuring instrument (and the applicable OIML Recommendation) from Scheme A back to Scheme B,

iii. extending or reducing the two-year transition period for a category of measuring instrument when moving from Scheme B to Scheme A, and

iv. withdrawing previous editions of an OIML Recommendation from the OIML-CS (see 15.5).
