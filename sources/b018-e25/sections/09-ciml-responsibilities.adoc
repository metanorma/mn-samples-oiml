== Responsibilities of the CIML within the OIML-CS

The CIML is responsible for

. appointing the Chairperson of the Management Committee,

. appointing the Deputy Chairperson of the Management Committee,

. appointing the Chairperson and members of the Board of Appeal,

. approving revisions to B 18, and

. approving proposals of the Management Committee concerning

.. including a new category of measuring instrument in the OIML-CS,

.. moving a category of measuring instrument (and the applicable OIML Recommendation) from Scheme A back to Scheme B,

.. extending or reducing the two-year transition period for a category of measuring instrument when moving from Scheme B to Scheme A, and

.. withdrawing previous editions of an OIML Recommendation from the OIML-CS (see <<sec_15.5>>).
