.Foreword

This publication has been prepared by the Maintenance Group of the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS) Management Committee.

The Management Committee approved this Edition 6 of OIML-CS PD-05 at its Ninth meeting on 06 March 2024 and it was published on 11 March 2024. This edition takes effect immediately upon publication.

Amendment 1 was issued on 27 August 2024 to correct an error in 5.4.1. The original text was:

[quote]
5.4.1 The provisions defined in 4.4.1, 4.4.3, 4.4.4 and 4.4.5 above apply.

The text has been amended to read:

[quote]
5.4.1 The provisions defined in 4.4.1, 4.4.3, 4.4.4, 4.4.5 and 4.4.6 above apply.

This publication is directly related to OIML B 18 _Framework for the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS)_ <<OIML_B_18>> which contains the framework for the operation of the OIML-CS.
