[[cls-8]]
== Performance tests related to influence factors and disturbances

=== General requirements (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.1>>)

==== Overview

This clause defines the performance tests which are intended to verify that
water meters perform and function as intended in a specified environment and
under specified conditions. Each test indicates, where appropriate, the reference
conditions for determining the intrinsic error.

These performance tests are additional to the tests specified in <<cls-7>> and
apply to complete meters, to separable parts of a water meter, and, if required,
to ancillary devices. The tests are required depending on the environmental
or electromagnetic class of meter as specified in <<scls-8-1-2>> and <<scls-8-1-3>>and
the type of meter construction or design as specified in <<scls-8-1-8>>.

When the effect of one influence quantity is being evaluated, all other influence
quantities are to be held at the reference conditions (see <<cls-4>>).

The type evaluation tests specified in this clause may be carried out in parallel
with the tests specified in <<cls-7>>, using examples of the same model of the
water meter, or its separable parts.

[[scls-8-1-2]]
==== Environmental classification

For each performance test, typical test conditions are indicated; they correspond
to the mechanical and climatic environmental conditions to which water meters
are exposed: see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.2>>.

[[scls-8-1-3]]
==== Electromagnetic classification

Water meters with electronic devices are divided into two electromagnetic environmental
classes: E1 for instruments operating in protected areas; and E2 for instruments
operating in areas without any special protection. See <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.3>>.

==== Reference conditions (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=7.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=7.1>>)

Reference conditions are listed in <<cls-4>>.

These reference conditions should only be applied if no reference conditions
are specified by a relevant regional or national standard designed to meet specific
conditions. If specified by such a standard, then the criteria contained therein
should be applied.

==== Test volumes for measuring error (of indication) of a water meter

Some influence quantities should have a constant effect on the error of indication
of a water meter and not a proportional effect related to the measured volume.

In other tests, the effect of the influence quantity applied to a water meter
is related to the measured volume. In these cases, in order to be able to compare
results obtained in different laboratories, the test volume for measuring the
error of indication of the meter shall correspond to that delivered in
stem:[1 "unitsml(min)"] at the overload flow rate stem:[Q_4].

However, some tests may require more than stem:[1 "unitsml(min)"], in which
case they shall be carried out in the shortest possible time, taking into consideration
the measurement uncertainty.

==== Influence of the water temperature (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

Dry heat, cold, and damp heat tests are concerned with measuring the effects
of ambient air temperature on the performance of the meter. However, the presence
of the measurement transducer, filled with water, may also influence heat dissipation
in electronic components.

There are two options for the testing.

. [[item-8-1-6-a]]The meter has water passing through it at the reference flow rate and the
error (of indication) of the meter is measured with the electronic parts and
the measurement transducer subjected to the reference conditions.
. A simulation of the measurement transducer is used for testing all electronic
components. These simulated tests shall replicate the effects caused by the
presence of water for those electronic devices which are normally attached to
the flow sensor, and the reference conditions shall be applied during the tests.

Option <<item-8-1-6-a>> is recommended.

==== Requirements for environmental tests

The following requirements are associated with the environmental tests and the
relevant IEC standards to be applied are listed in the appropriate subclauses
of this part of ISO 4064|OIML R 49:

. preconditioning of the equipment under test (EUT);
. any deviations in the procedure from the relevant IEC standard;
. initial measurements;
. state of the EUT during conditioning;
. severity levels, values of the influence factor and duration of exposure;
. measurements required and/or the loading during conditioning;
. recovery of the EUT;
. final measurements;
. acceptance criteria for the EUT passing a test.

Where no IEC standard exists for a specific test, the essential requirements
for the test are given in this part of ISO 4064|OIML R 49.

==== Equipment under test (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=7.2.12.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=7.2.12.3>>)

===== General

For the purpose of testing, the EUT shall be categorised as one of the cases,
A to E, according to the technology specified in <<scls-8-1-8-2>> to <<scls-8-1-8-5>>
and the following requirements shall apply:

Case A:: No performance test (as mentioned in this subclause) is required.

Case B:: The EUT is the complete meter or combined meter: the test shall be
carried out with water flowing in the volume or flow sensor and the meter operating
as designed.

Case C:: The EUT is the measurement transducer (including the flow or volume
sensor): the test shall be carried out with water flowing in the volume or flow
sensor and the meter operating as designed.

Case D:: The EUT is the electronic calculator (including the indicating device)
or the ancillary device: the test shall be carried out with water flowing in
the volume or flow sensor and the meter operating as designed.

Case E:: The EUT is the electronic calculator (including the indicating device)
or the ancillary device: the test may be carried out with simulated measurement
signals without water in the volume or flow sensor.

The body responsible for type approval may apply an appropriate category, A
to E, for approval testing of meters having technology which is not included
in <<scls-8-1-8-2>> to <<scls-8-1-8-5>>.

. The electronic calculator including the indicating device is separate from
the measurement transducer and simulation of the measurement signals is not
possible: Case D
. The electronic calculator including the indicating device is separate from

Case E

===== Ultrasonic water meters, Coriolis water meters, fluidic water meters

. The measurement transducer and the electronic calculator including the indicating

. The measurement transducer is separate from the electronic calculator and
fitted with electronic devices: Case C
. The electronic calculator including the indicating device is separate from
the measurement transducer and simulation of the measurement signals is not

. The electronic calculator including the indicating device is separate from

Case E

. The ancillary device is part of the water meter, a part of the measurement

. The ancillary device is separate from the meter, a simulation of the input

=== Dry heat (non-condensing) (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>, during the application of high ambient temperatures as in
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,annex=A,table=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,annex=A,table=A.1>>.

==== Preparation

Follow the test arrangements specified in <<IEC_60068_2_2_2007>>.

Guidance on testing arrangements is given in <<IEC_60068_3_1_2023>> and <<IEC_60068_1_2013>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. No pre-conditioning is required.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate and
at the following test conditions:
.. at the reference air temperature of stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"],
before conditioning the EUT;
.. at an air temperature of stem:[55 "unitsml(degC)" pm 2 "unitsml(degC)"],
after the EUT has been stabilised at this temperature for a period of
stem:[2 "unitsml(h)"];
.. at the reference air temperature of stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"],
after recovery of the EUT.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. During the application of the test conditions, check that the EUT is functioning
correctly.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[start=6]
. If the measurement transducer is included in the EUT, and it is necessary
to have water in the flow sensor, the water temperature shall be held at the
reference temperature.
. When measuring the errors (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two
reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

During the application of the test conditions:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. the relative error (of indication) of the EUT, at the test conditions, shall
not exceed the maximum permissible error of the upper flow rate zone (see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>).

=== Cold (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>, during the application of low ambient temperatures as in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,annex=A,table=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,annex=A,table=A.1>>.

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_60068_2_1_2007>>.

Guidance on testing arrangements is given in <<IEC_60068_3_1_2023>> and <<IEC_60068_1_2013>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. Do not pre-condition the EUT.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate and
at the reference air temperature.
. Stabilise the air temperature at either stem:[-25 "unitsml(degC)" pm 3 "unitsml(degC)"]
(environmental classes O and M) or stem:[+5 "unitsml(degC)" pm 3 "unitsml(degC)"]
(environmental class B) for a period of stem:[2 "unitsml(h)"].
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate at
an air temperature of either stem:[-25 "unitsml(degC)" pm 3 "unitsml(degC)"]
(environmental classes O and M) or stem:[+5 "unitsml(degC)" pm 3 "unitsml(degC)"]
(environmental class B).
. After recovery of the EUT, measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at
the reference flow rate and at the reference air temperature.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. During the application of the test conditions, check that the EUT is functioning
correctly.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.2>>.

Additional requirements follow.

. If it is necessary to have water in the flow sensor, the water temperature
shall be held at the reference temperature.
. When measuring the errors (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two
reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

During the application of the stabilised test conditions:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed; and
. the relative error (of indication) of the EUT, at the test conditions, shall
not exceed the maximum permissible error of the upper flow rate zone (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>).

=== Damp heat, cyclic (condensing) (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>, after applying conditions of high humidity, combined with
cyclic temperature changes as in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>.

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_60068_2_30_2005>>.

Guidance on testing arrangements is given in <<IEC_60068_3_4_2023>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

Follow the requirements for the performance of the test equipment, conditioning
and recovery of the EUT, and exposure of the EUT to cyclic temperature changes
under damp heat conditions specified in <<IEC_60068_2_30_2005>> and <<IEC_60068_3_4_2023>>.

The test program consists of steps <<item-8-4-3-a>> to <<item-8-4-3-g>>.

. [[item-8-4-3-a]]Pre-condition the EUT.
. Expose the EUT to cyclic temperature variations between the lower temperature
of stem:[25 "unitsml(degC)" pm 3 "unitsml(degC)"] and the upper temperature
of stem:[55 "unitsml(degC)" pm 2 "unitsml(degC)"] (environmental classes O and
M) or stem:[40 "unitsml(degC)" pm 2 "unitsml(degC)"] (environmental class B).
Maintain the relative humidity above stem:[95 %] during the temperature changes
and during the phases at low temperature, and at stem:[93 % pm 3 %] at the upper
temperature phases. Condensation should occur on the EUT during the temperature rise.
+
--
The stem:[24 "unitsml(h)"] cycle consists of:

.. temperature rise over stem:[3 "unitsml(h)"];
.. temperature maintained at upper value until stem:[12 "unitsml(h)"] from the
start of the cycle;
.. temperature taken down to the lower value within stem:[3 "unitsml(h)"] to
stem:[6 "unitsml(h)"], the rate of fall during the first stem:[1 "unitsml(h)"]
stem:[30 "unitsml(min)"] being such that the lower value would be reached in
stem:[3 "unitsml(h)"];
.. temperature maintained at lower value until the stem:[24 "unitsml(h)"] cycle
is completed.
--
.  [[item-8-4-3-c]]Allow the EUT to recover.
. After recovery, check that the EUT is functioning correctly.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) in accordance with <<annex-b>>.
. [[item-8-4-3-g]]Complete the test report in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.3>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[start=8]
. The power supply to the EUT shall be switched off during steps <<item-8-4-3-a>>
to <<item-8-4-3-c>>.
. The stabilising period before and recovery after the cyclic exposure shall
be such that all parts of the EUT are within stem:[3 "unitsml(degC)"] of their
final temperature.
. When measuring the error (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two
reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

After the application of the disturbance and recovery:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. either the difference between any indication before the test and the indication
after the test shall not exceed one-half of the maximum permissible error in
the upper flow rate zone or the EUT shall detect and act upon a significant
fault, in compliance with <<ISO_4064_1_2024,annex=B>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,annex=B>>.

=== Power supply variation (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Water meters powered by direct AC or by AC/DC converters (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

===== Object of the test

To verify that electronic devices which operate at a nominal value of mains
voltage, stem:[U_"nom"], at a nominal frequency, stem:[f_"nom"], comply with
the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>, during static deviations
of the AC (single-phase) mains power supply, applied in accordance with the
requirements of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>.

===== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_61000_4_11_2020>>, <<IEC_61000_2_1_1990>>,
<<IEC_61000_2_2_2002>>, <<IEC_61000_4_1_2016>>, and <<IEC_60654_2_1979>>.

===== Test procedure (in brief)

. Expose the EUT to power voltage variations and subsequently to power supply
frequency variations, while the EUT is operating under reference conditions.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of the
upper mains voltage limit, stem:[U_"nom" + 10 %] (single voltage).
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of the
upper mains frequency limit, stem:[f_"nom" + 2 %].
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of the
lower mains voltage limit, stem:[U_"nom" - 15 %] (single voltage).
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of the
lower mains frequency limit, stem:[f_"nom" - 2 %].
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. Check that the EUT is functioning correctly during the application of each
power supply variation.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.2>>.

Additional requirements follow.

. During the measurement of the error (of indication), the EUT shall be subjected
to the reference flow rate (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=7.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=7.1>>).
. When measuring the errors (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two
reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.

===== Acceptance criteria

During the application of the influence factor:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. the relative error of indication of the EUT, at the test conditions, shall
not exceed the maximum permissible error of the upper flow rate zone
(see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>).

==== Water meters powered by external DC voltage or by primary DC batteries (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

===== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>> during static deviations of the DC power supply voltage, applied

===== Test procedure

reference conditions.

detect high-level conditions for an external DC supply.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT, during the application of the
minimum operating voltage of the battery, as specified by the water meter supplier,
for a battery or the DC voltage at which the EUT has been manufactured to automatically
detect low-level conditions for an external DC supply.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. Check that EUT is functioning correctly during the application of each power
supply variation.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.3>>.

Additional requirements follow.

. When measuring the errors (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two

==== Interruption in battery supply

===== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the requirements in
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.2.4.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.2.4.3>>,
during replacement of the supply battery.

This test only applies to meters utilising a replaceable battery supply.

===== Test procedure

. Ensure that the meter is operational.
. Remove the battery for a period of stem:[1 "unitsml(h)"] and then reconnect it.
. Interrogate the functions of the meter.
. Complete the section reference in
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.2.4>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.2.4>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.4.3>>.

===== Acceptance criteria

After the application of the test conditions:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. the value of the totalisation or the stored values shall remain unchanged.

=== Vibration (random) (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>, after the application of random vibrations (see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.1>>).

This test is applicable only to meters for mobile installations (environmental
class M).

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_60068_2_64_2008_AMD1_2019_CSV>>
and <<IEC_60068_2_47_2005>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. [[item-8-6-3-a]]Mount the EUT on a rigid fixture by its normal mounting means, such that the
gravitational force acts in the same direction as it would in normal use. However,
if the gravitational effect is insignificant, and the meter is not marked "H"
or "V", the EUT may be mounted in any position.
. [[item-8-6-3-b]]Apply random vibrations over the frequency range of stem:[10 "unitsml(Hz)"]
to stem:[150 "unitsml(Hz)"] to the EUT, in three, mutually perpendicular axes
in turn, for a period of at least stem:[2 "unitsml(min)"] per axis.
. [[item-8-6-3-c]]Allow the EUT a period for recovery.
. Examine the EUT for correct functioning.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) in accordance with <<annex-b>>.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.5>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[start=8]
. Where the flow sensor is included in the EUT, it shall not be filled with
water during the application of the disturbance.
. The power supply to the EUT is switched off during steps <<item-8-6-3-a>>, <<item-8-6-3-b>>
and <<item-8-6-3-c>>.
. During the application of the vibrations the following conditions shall be met:
* Total RMS level: stem:[7 "unitsml(m/s^2)"];
* acceleration spectral density (ASD) level stem:[10 "unitsml(Hz)"] to
stem:[20 "unitsml(Hz)"]: stem:[1 "unitsml(m^2/s^3)"];
* ASD level stem:[20 "unitsml(Hz)"] to stem:[150 "unitsml(Hz)"]: stem:[-3 "dB/octave"].
. When measuring the errors (of indication) of the EUT, the installation and
operational conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the
reference conditions shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters
not marked "V" shall be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation.
Meters with two reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference
temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

After the application of the vibrations and recovery:

. all functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. either the difference between any indication before the test and the indication
after the test shall not exceed one-half of the maximum permissible error in
the upper flow rate zone or the EUT shall detect and act upon a significant
fault, in compliance with <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=B>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=B>>.

=== Mechanical shock (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>, after the application of the mechanical shock test (dropping
on to face) as in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.1>>.

This test is applicable only to meters for mobile installations (environmental
class M).

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_60068_2_31_2008>> and <<IEC_60068_2_47_2005>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. [[item-8-7-3-a]]The EUT shall be placed on a rigid, level surface in its normal position of
use and tilted towards one bottom edge until the opposite edge of the EUT is
stem:[50 "unitsml(mm)"] above the rigid surface. However, the angle made by
the bottom of the EUT and the test surface shall not exceed stem:[30 "unitsml(deg)"].
. [[item-8-7-3-b]]Allow the EUT to fall freely on to the test surface.
. [[item-8-7-3-c]]Repeat steps <<item-8-7-3-a>> and <<item-8-7-3-b>> for each bottom edge.
. Allow the EUT a period for recovery.
. Examine the EUT for correct functioning.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT at the reference flow rate.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) in accordance with <<annex-b>>.
. Complete the test report in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.6>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.6>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[lowerroman]
. Where the flow sensor is part of the EUT, it shall not be filled with water
during the application of the disturbance.
. The power supply to the EUT shall be switched off during steps <<item-8-7-3-a>>,
<<item-8-7-3-b>>, and <<item-8-7-3-c>>.
. When measuring the errors (of indication) of the EUT, the installation and
operational conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the
reference conditions shall be applied. Test meters not marked "V" shall be mounted
with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two reference
temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

After the application of the disturbance and recovery:

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
. either the difference between any indication before the test and the indication
after the test shall not exceed one-half of the maximum permissible error in
the upper flow rate zone or the EUT shall detect and act upon a significant
fault, in compliance with <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=B>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=B>>.

=== Bursts on signal lines (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter containing electronics and provided with input/output
(I/O) and communication ports (including its external cables) complies with
the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>, under conditions
where electrical bursts are superimposed on I/O and communication port as in
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,table=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,table=A.1>>.

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_61000_4_4_2012>> and <<IEC_61000_4_1_2016>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT before applying the electrical bursts.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of bursts
of transient voltage spikes, of double exponential waveform.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. Subtract the error (of indication) of the meter measured before applying the
bursts from that measured during the application of the bursts.
. Examine the EUT for correct functioning.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.8>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.8>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[start=7]
. A burst generator shall be used with the performance characteristics as specified
in the cited standard.
. The characteristics of the generator shall be verified before connecting to
the EUT.
. Each spike shall have an amplitude (positive or negative) of stem:[0.5 "unitsml(kV)"]
for environmental class E1 instruments or stem:[1 "unitsml(kV)"] for environmental
class E2 instruments (see <<scls-8-1-3>>), phased randomly, with a rise time
of stem:[5 "unitsml(ns)"] and a half amplitude duration of stem:[50 "unitsml(ns)"].
. The burst length shall be stem:[15 "unitsml(ms)"] and the burst repetition
rate shall be stem:[5 "unitsml(kHz)"].
. The injection network on the mains shall contain blocking filters to prevent
the burst energy from being dissipated in the mains.
. For the coupling of the bursts into the I/O and communication lines, a capacitive
coupling clamp as defined in the standard shall be used.
. The duration of the test shall not be less than stem:[1 "unitsml(min)"] for
each amplitude and polarity.
. During the measurement of the error (of indication) the EUT shall be operated
at the reference flow rate.
. When measuring the error (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions of the EUT, specified in <<scls-7-4-2>>, shall be followed and the
reference conditions shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters
not marked "V" shall be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation.
Meters with two reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference
temperature.

==== Acceptance criteria

. After the application of the disturbance, all the functions of the EUT shall
operate as designed.
. The difference between the relative error of indication, obtained during the
application of the bursts and that obtained at the same flow rate before the
test, under reference conditions, shall not exceed one-half of the maximum permissible
see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>) or the
EUT shall detect and act upon a significant fault in compliance with
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=B>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=B>>.

=== Electrostatic discharge (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=A.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=A.5>>)

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter complies with the provisions of <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=5.1.1>> during the application of direct and indirect electrostatic
discharges as in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,table=A.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,table=A.1>>.

==== Preparation

Follow the testing arrangements specified in <<IEC_61000_4_2_2008>>.

==== Test procedure (in brief)

. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT before applying the electrostatic
discharges.
. Charge a stem:[150 "unitsml(pF)"] capacitor by means of a suitable DC voltage
source, then discharge the capacitor through the EUT by connecting one terminal
of the supporting chassis to earth and the other via a stem:[330 "unitsml(Ohm)"]
resistor to surfaces of the EUT which are normally accessible to the operator.
. The following conditions shall be applied:
.. include the paint penetration method, if appropriate;
.. for each contact discharge, a voltage of stem:[6 "unitsml(kV)"] shall be applied;
.. for each air discharge, a voltage of stem:[8 "unitsml(kV)"] shall be applied;
.. for direct discharges, the air discharge method shall be used where the manufacturer
has declared a coating to be insulating;
.. at each test location, at least 10 direct discharges shall be applied at
intervals of at least stem:[10 "unitsml(s)"] between discharges, during the
same measurement or simulated measurement;
.. for indirect discharges, a total of 10 discharges shall be applied on the
horizontal coupling plane and a total of 10 discharges for each of the various
positions of the vertical coupling plane.
. Measure the error (of indication) of the EUT during the application of electrostatic
discharges.
. Calculate the relative error (of indication) for each test condition in accordance
with <<annex-b>>.
. Determine if the significant fault has been exceeded by subtracting the error
(of indication) of the meter measured before applying the electrostatic discharges
from that measured after applying the electrostatic discharges.
. Examine the EUT for correct functioning.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.10>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=4.4.10>>.

Additional requirements follow.

[start=9]
. When measuring the error (of indication) the EUT shall be subjected to the
reference flow rate.
. When measuring the error (of indication), the installation and operational
conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Test meters not marked "V" shall
be mounted with the flow axis in the horizontal orientation. Meters with two
reference temperatures are only tested at the lower reference temperature.
. In cases where a specific meter design is expected to be no less susceptible
to disturbances at zero flow rate than if operated at the reference conditions
for flow rate, the body responsible for type approval shall be free to choose
a flow rate of zero during the electrostatic discharge test.
. For EUT not equipped with an earth terminal, the EUT shall be fully discharged
between discharges.
. Contact discharge is the preferred test method. Air discharges shall be used
where contact discharge cannot be applied.
.. Direct application
+
--
In the contact discharge mode to be carried out on conductive surfaces, the
electrode shall be in contact with the EUT.

In the air discharge mode on insulated surfaces, the electrode is moved towards
the EUT and the discharge occurs by spark.
--
.. Indirect application
+
--
The discharges are applied in the contact mode to coupling planes mounted in
the vicinity of the EUT.
--

==== Acceptance criteria

. After the application of the disturbance, all the functions of the EUT shall
operate as designed.
. The difference between the relative error (of indication), obtained during
the application of the electrostatic discharges and that obtained before the
test, at the same flow rate, under reference conditions, shall not exceed one-half
of the maximum permissible error in the upper flow rate zone (see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=4.2>>) or the EUT shall detect and act upon a significant fault in
compliance with <<ISO_4064_1_2024,annex=B>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,annex=B>>.
. For tests at zero flow rate, the water meter totalisation shall not change
by more than the value of the least significant digit of the indicating device.

==== Object of the test

To verify that a water meter – with electronic components and/or where mechanical

The water meter shall be made operational in accordance with the rated operating

==== Test procedure in brief

. The permanent magnet is placed in contact with the EUT at a position where

and construction of the EUT, and/or previous experience. Different positions
of the magnet may be investigated.

and the error (of indication) of the EUT is measured at flow rate, stem:[Q_3].

conditions specified in <<scls-7-4-2>> shall be followed and the reference conditions
shall be applied unless otherwise specified. Meters not marked "V" shall only

temperatures shall only be tested at the lowest reference temperature.

be measured and recorded for each test position.

. all the functions of the EUT shall operate as designed;
