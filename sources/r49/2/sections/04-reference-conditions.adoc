== Reference conditions

All applicable influence quantities, except for the influence quantity being
tested, shall be held at the following values during type evaluation tests on
a water meter. However, for influence factors and disturbances for electronic
water meters, it is permissible to use the reference conditions defined in the
applicable IEC standard:

Flow rate:: stem:[0.7 xx (Q_2 + Q_3) pm 0.03 xx (Q_2 + Q_3)]

Water temperature:: T30, T50 is stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"] +
T70 to T180 is stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"] and
stem:[50 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"] +
T30/70 to T30/180 is stem:[50 "unitsml(degC)" pm 5 "unitsml(degC)"]

Water pressure:: Within rated operating conditions +
(see <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.4>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.4>>)

Ambient temperature range:: stem:[15 "unitsml(degC)"] to stem:[25 "unitsml(degC)"]

Ambient relative humidity range:: stem:[25 %] to stem:[75 %]

Ambient atmospheric pressure range:: stem:[86 "unitsml(kPa)"] to
stem:[106 "unitsml(kPa)"] [stem:[0.86 "unitsml(bar)"] to stem:[1.06 "unitsml(bar)"]]

Power supply voltage (mains AC):: Nominal voltage, stem:[U_"nom" pm 5 %]

Power supply frequency:: Nominal frequency, stem:[f_"nom" pm 2 %]

Power supply voltage (battery):: A voltage stem:[V] in the range
stem:[U_"bmin" <= V <= U_"bmax"]

During each test, the temperature and relative humidity shall not vary by more
than stem:[5 "unitsml(degC)"] or stem:[10 %], respectively, within the reference
range. The reference conditions are permitted to deviate from the defined tolerance
values during the performance tests if evidence can be given to the body responsible
for type approval that the type of meter under consideration is not affected
by the deviation of the condition in question. The actual values of the deviating
condition, however, shall be measured and documented as part of the performance
test documentation.
