[[cls-6]]
== External examination

=== General

During the external examination, all relevant values, dimensions, and observations
shall be recorded.

NOTE: For presentation of the results of type examinations, see <<cls-11>>.

NOTE: The relevant subclauses of <<ISO_4064_1_2024>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024>> are shown
in parentheses in the following.

=== Object of the examination

To verify that a water meter meets the requirements of <<ISO_4064_1_2024>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024>> with respect to the design of the indicating device, the marking
of the meter and the application of protection devices.

=== Preparation

Linear measurements that have to be taken of a meter shall be made using traceable,
calibrated measuring devices.

The actual or apparent dimensions of the scales of the indicating device shall
be taken without removing the meter lens or disassembling the meter.

NOTE: A travelling microscope (cathetometer) can be used to measure the width,
spacing and height of the scale divisions and the height of the numerals.

=== Examination procedures

==== General

The following aspects of a meter design shall be examined on at least one meter
from the sample.

Either the same meter sample may be used for all the external examinations or
different meters from the samples submitted may be used for some of the examinations.

==== Marks and inscriptions (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.6>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.6>>)

. Verify that a place is provided for affixing the verification mark which is
visible without dismantling the water meter.
. Verify that the water meter is clearly and indelibly marked with the information
presented in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.6.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.6.2>>.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.6.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.6.1>> and
6.6.2 (r) in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

[[scls-6-4-3]]
==== Indicating device (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7>>)

===== Function (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.1>>)

. Verify that the indicating device provides an easily read, reliable and unambiguous
visual indication of the indicated volume.
. Verify that the indicating device includes visual means for testing and calibration.
. If the indicating device includes additional elements for testing and calibration
by other methods, e.g. for automatic testing and calibration, record the type(s)
of device.
. If the meter is a combination meter with two indicating devices, <<scls-6-4-3>>
applies to both indicating devices.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.1>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

===== Unit of measurement, symbol, and its placement (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.2>>)

. Verify that the indicated volume of water is expressed in cubic metres.
. Verify that the symbol stem:["unitsml(m^3)"] appears on the dial or immediately
adjacent to the numbered display.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.2>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

===== Indicating range (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.3>>)

. Verify that the indicating device is able to record the indicated volume in
cubic metres given in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,table=5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,table=5>> corresponding
to the permanent flow rate stem:[Q_3] without passing through zero.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.3>>,
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

===== Colour coding for indicating devices (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.4>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.4>>)

. Verify that either:
.. the colour black is used to indicate the cubic metre and its multiples; and
.. the colour red is used to indicate sub-multiples of a cubic metre; and
.. the colours are applied either to the pointers, indexes, numbers, wheels
discs, dials or aperture frames;
.. or other means of indicating the cubic metre are used in which there is no
ambiguity in distinguishing between the primary indication and alternative displays,
e.g. sub-multiples for verification and testing.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.4>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.1.4>>,
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

===== Types of indicating device (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2>>)

[[scls-6-4-3-5-1]]
====== Type 1 -- Analogue device (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.1>>)

. If a type 1 indicating device has been used, verify that volume is indicated by:
* either continuous movement of one or more pointers moving relative to graduated
scales;
* or continuous movement of one or more circular scales or drums, each passing
an index.
. Verify that the value expressed in cubic metres for each scale division is
of the form stem:[10^n], where stem:[n] is a positive or a negative whole number
or zero, thereby establishing a system of consecutive decades.
. Verify that each scale is either graduated in values expressed in cubic metres
or accompanied by a multiplication factor (stem:[xx 0.001]; stem:[xx 0.01];
stem:[xx 1]; stem:[xx 10]; stem:[xx 100]; stem:[xx 1000], etc.).
. Verify that the rotational movements of the pointers or circular scales are
clockwise.
. Verify that the linear movement of the pointers or scales is from left to right.
. Verify that the movement of the numbered roller indicators is upwards.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.1>>,
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

[[scls-6-4-3-5-2]]
====== Type 2 -- Digital device (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.2>>)

. Verify that the indicated volume is given by a line of digits, appearing in
one or more apertures.
. Verify that the advance of one digit is completed while the digit of the next
immediately lower decade changes from 9 to 0.
. Verify that the actual or apparent height of the digits is at least
stem:[4 "unitsml(mm)"].
. For non-electronic devices:
+
--
. verify that the movement of the numbered roller indicators (drums) is upwards;
. if the lowest value decade has a continuous movement, verify that the aperture
is large enough to permit a digit to be read without ambiguity.

For electronic devices:

. verify that for non-permanent displays the volume can be displayed at any
time for at least stem:[10 "unitsml(s)"];
. check the entire display visually in the following sequence:
.. for seven segment type verify that all the elements can be correctly displayed
(e.g. an "eights" test),
.. for seven segment type verify that all the elements can be blanked (a "blanks"
test),
.. for graphical displays use an equivalent test to verify that display faults
cannot result in any digit being misinterpreted,
.. verify that each step of the sequence lasts at least stem:[1 "unitsml(s)"].
--
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.2>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

====== Type 3 -- Combination of analogue and digital devices (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.3>>)

. If the indicating device is a combination of type 1 and 2 devices, verify
that the respective requirements of each apply (see <<scls-6-4-3-5-1>> and
<<scls-6-4-3-5-2>>).
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.2.3>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

===== Verification devices -- First element of an indicating device -- Verification scale interval (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3>>)

====== General requirements (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.1>>)

. Verify that the indicating device has the means for visual, non-ambiguous
verification testing and calibration.
. Note whether the visual verification display has a continuous or a discontinuous
movement.
. Note whether, in addition to the visual verification display, the indicating
device includes provisions for rapid testing by the inclusion of complementary
elements (e.g. star wheels or discs), providing signals through externally attached
sensors. Note the relationship, stated by the manufacturer, between the visual
indication of volume and the signals emitted by these complementary devices.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.1>> in
<<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

====== Visual verification display (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2>>)

[level=6]
====== Value of the verification scale interval (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.1>>)

. Verify that the value of the verification scale interval, expressed in cubic
metres, is of the form stem:[1 xx 10^n], or stem:[2 xx 10^n], or stem:[5 xx 10^n],
where stem:[n] is a positive or negative whole number, or zero.
. For analogue and digital indicating devices with continuous movement of the
first element, verify that the verification scale interval is formed from the
division into 2, 5 or 10 equal parts of the interval between two consecutive
digits of the first element.
. For analogue and digital indicating devices with continuous movement of the
first element, verify that numbering is not applied to the divisions between
consecutive digits of the first element.
. For digital indicating devices with discontinuous movement of the first element,
the verification scale interval is the interval between two consecutive digits
or incremental movements of the first element.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.1>>
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

[level=6]
====== Form of the verification scale (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.2>>)

. If the indicating device has continuous movement of the first element, check
that the apparent scale spacing is not less than stem:[1 "unitsml(mm)"] and
not more than stem:[5 "unitsml(mm)"].
. Verify that the scale consists of:
* either lines of equal thickness not exceeding one-quarter of the scale spacing
and differing only in length;
* or contrasting bands of a constant width equal to the scale spacing.
. Verify that the apparent width of the pointer at its tip does not exceed one-quarter
of the scale spacing.
. Verify that the apparent width of the pointer at its tip does not exceed
stem:[0.5 "unitsml(mm)"].
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.2>>,
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

[level=6]
====== Resolution of the indicating device (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.3>>)

. Note the value of the verification scale interval, stem:[delta V "unitsml(m^3)"]
. Calculate the actual volume stem:[V_a] in stem:["unitsml(m^3)"] passed during
stem:[90 "unitsml(min)"] at the minimum flow rate stem:[Q_1], from
+
[stem]
++++
V_a = Q_1 xx 1.5
++++
. Calculate the resolution error stem:[epsilon_r] of the indicating device,
expressed as a percentage, from:
.. for continuous movement of the first element:
+
[stem]
++++
epsilon_r = frac(0,5 delta V + 0,5 delta V)(V_a) xx 100 % = frac(delta V)(V_a) xx 100 %
++++
.. For discontinuous movement of the first element:
+
[stem]
++++
epsilon_r = frac(delta V + delta V)(V_a) xx 100 % = frac(2 delta V)(V_a) xx 100 %
++++
. Verify that for accuracy class 1 meters, the value of the verification scale
interval is small enough to ensure that the resolution error stem:[epsilon_r]
of the indicating device does not exceed stem:[0.25 %] of the actual volume
required during stem:[90 "unitsml(min)"] at the minimum flow rate, stem:[Q_1].
+
[stem]
++++
epsilon_r <= 0.25 %
++++
. Verify that for accuracy class 2 meters, the verification scale interval is
small enough to ensure that the resolution error stem:[epsilon_r] of the indicating
device does not exceed stem:[0.5 %] of the actual volume required during
stem:[90 "unitsml(min)"] at the minimum flow rate, stem:[Q_1].
+
[stem]
++++
epsilon_r <= 0.5 %
++++
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.7.3.2.3>>
in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.

When the display of the first element is continuous, an allowance shall be made
for a maximum error in each reading of not more than half of the verification
scale interval.

When the display of the first element is discontinuous, an allowance shall be
made for a maximum error in each reading of not more than one digit of the verification
scale.

==== Protection devices (<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.8>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.8>>)

. Verify that the water meter includes protection devices as specified in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.8>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.8>>.
. Complete the section reference <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.8.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.8.1>> and
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=6.8.2.3>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=6.8.2.3>> in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=4.4.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_4_2024,clause=3.1>>.
