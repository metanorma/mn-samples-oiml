
[[cls-10]]
== Tests for initial verification

[[scls-10-1]]
=== Initial verification of complete and combined water meters

For the initial verification national regulations may apply.

==== Object of the test

To verify that the relative errors (of indication) of a complete or combined
water meter are within the maximum permissible errors given in
<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause="4.2.2 or 4.2.3">>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause="4.2.2 or 4.2.3">>.

The reference conditions are permitted to deviate from the defined tolerance
values during the verification tests if evidence can be given to the body responsible
for type approval that the type of meter under consideration is not affected
by the deviation of the condition in question. The actual values of the deviating
condition, however, have to be measured and documented as part of the verification
test documentation.

==== Preparation

A pressure test shall be performed at 1.6 times the MAP for stem:[1 "unitsml(min)"].

During the test no leaks shall be observed.

The errors (of indication) of the water meter shall be measured using equipment
and principles specified in <<scls-7-2>>and <<scls-7-4>>.

[[scls-10-1-3]]
==== Test procedure

. Install the meters for testing either singly or in series.
. Apply the procedures given in <<scls-7-4>>.
. Ensure that there is no significant interaction between meters installed in series.
. Ensure that the outlet pressure of any meter is not less than stem:[0.03 "unitsml(MPa)"]
(stem:[0.3 "unitsml(bar)"]).
. Ensure that working water temperature range is as follows:
* T30, T50: stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 10 "unitsml(degC)"];
* T70 to T180: stem:[20 "unitsml(degC)" pm 10 "unitsml(degC)"] and
stem:[50 "unitsml(degC)" pm 10 "unitsml(degC)"];
* T30/70 to T30/180: stem:[50 "unitsml(degC)" pm 10 "unitsml(degC)"].
. Ensure that all other influence factors are held within the rated operating
conditions of the meter.
. [[item-10-1-3-g]]Unless alternative flow rates are specified in the
type approval certificate, measure the errors (of indication) at the following
flow rate ranges:
+
--
* stem:[Q_1] to stem:[1.1 Q_1];
* stem:[Q_2] to stem:[1.1 Q_2];
* stem:[0.9 Q_3] to stem:[Q_3];
* for combination meters, stem:[1.05 Q_(x2)] to stem:[1.15 Q_(x2)].

NOTE: See also <<item-10-1-4-c>>.
--
. Calculate the error (of indication) for each flow rate in accordance with
<<annex-b>>.
. Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=5.3.1>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=5.3.1>>, Example 1.

[[scls-10-1-4]]
==== Acceptance criteria

. The errors (of indication) of the water meter shall not exceed the maximum
permissible errors given in <<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=4.2.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause="4.2.2 or 4.2.3">>.
. [[item-10-1-4-b]]If all the errors (of indication) of the water meter have the same sign, within
the complete measuring range (stem:[Q_1] to stem:[Q_4]), the water meter shall
only pass initial verification if all errors do not exceed one-half of the maximum
permissible error. For non-adjustable water meters, statistical means may be
used to demonstrate that a batch of water meters complies with this requirement
when the error at the maximum of the error distribution, <<figure-4>>, does
not exceed one-half of the maximum permissible error at flowrate stem:[Q_2]
or stem:[Q_3].
+

<<ISO_4064_1_2024,clause=7.3.5>>|<<OIML_R_49_1_2024,clause=7.3.5>>, additional errors at flow rates specified

=== Initial verification of separable parts of a water meter

==== Object of the test

To verify that the errors (of indication) of a measurement transducer (including
volume or flow sensor) or the calculator (including indicating device) are within
the maximum permissible errors stated in the type approval certificate.

A measurement transducer (including flow or volume sensors) shall be subjected
to the initial verification tests listed in <<scls-10-1>>.

A calculator (including indicating devices) shall be subjected to the initial
verification tests listed in <<scls-10-1>>.

==== Preparation

The errors (of indication) of separable approved parts of a water meter shall
be measured using equipment and principles specified in <<scls-7-2>> and the
performance test requirements of <<scls-7-4>> shall be met where applicable.

Where possible, the test conditions applied during the type evaluation of a
complete water meter shall be applied to the separable parts of a water meter.
Where this is not possible for certain test conditions, simulated conditions,
of equivalent characteristics, severity, and duration, shall be applied.

==== Test procedure

The test procedure in <<scls-10-1-3>> shall be followed except where simulated
testing is necessary.

Complete the test report in <<ISO_4064_3_2024,clause=5.3.2>>|<<OIML_R_49_3_2024,clause=5.3.2>>, Example
2 and/or 5.3.3, Example 3.

==== Acceptance criteria

The errors (of indication) of separable parts of the water meter shall not exceed
the maximum permissible errors stated in the type approval certificate.
