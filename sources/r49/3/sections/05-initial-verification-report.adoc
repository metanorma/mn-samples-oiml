== Initial verification report

=== General

The specific format layout for reporting initial verifications and subsequent
verifications of water meters is left largely to the metrological authorities
and the individual organisations carrying out verification tests. However, the
report (records) shall contain the minimum information detailed in OIML R 49-1:2024,
7.3 and OIML R 49-2:2024, 11.2.2.

In addition to this, any special requirements and/or restrictions for initial
verification detailed in the type approval certificate for the EUT shall be
applied. A record of equipment and instrumentation used with calibration details
(see <<table-2>> ) shall be kept.

The following basic information should also be included in the verification
report (record) followed by the results of the tests (three examples of how
the report may be formatted are given below):
