== Publication of Lists of Experts

=== {blank}

When an applicant has been approved by the Management Committee to be a Legal Metrology Expert and/or Management System Expert, the Executive Secretary will update the ILAC-IAF-OIML lists of experts published on the OIML-CS pages of the OIML website.

=== {blank}

When an expert is included on the list of Management System Experts, the Executive Secretary will send a notification to ILAC and/or IAF as appropriate.
