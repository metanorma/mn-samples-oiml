.Foreword

This publication has been prepared by the Maintenance Group of the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS) Management Committee.

The Management Committee approved this Edition 3 of OIML-CS OD-02 at its Ninth meeting on 06 March 2024 and it was published on 11 March 2024. This edition takes effect immediately upon publication.

This publication is directly related to the Framework for the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS) (OIML B 18 <<OIML_B_18>>) which contains the framework for the operation of the OIML-CS.
