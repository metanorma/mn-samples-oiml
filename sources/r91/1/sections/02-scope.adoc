
== Scope

This Recommendation specifies the metrological and technical requirements, metrological
controls and test procedures for speed enforcement devices for the measurement
of traffic speed of vehicles on the road, hereafter referred to as "speed meters".
The Recommendation states the conditions that the speed meter shall satisfy
when results of speed measurement are to be used in legal proceedings. The Recommendation
does not cover the policy of setting speed limits or trigger limits for measurements.

The Recommendation covers both general requirements valid for all speed meters
and specific requirements valid only for certain categories of speed meters
(see <<cls-5>>).
