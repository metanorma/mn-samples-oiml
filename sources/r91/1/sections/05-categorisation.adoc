== Categorisation of speed meters

Speed meters are categorised according to:

[arabic]
. Mode of use:
.. Stationary speed meter (speed meter is not moving during the measurements);
.. Moving speed meter (speed meter is installed on a vehicle, which is moving
during the measurements).
. Principle of installation:
.. Mobile speed meter (can be moved between several positions);
.. Fixed speed meter (fixed at one position);
.. Portable speed meter (can be used from hand or tripod).
. Working principle of speed measurement:
.. Virtually instantaneous speed measurement:
*** Doppler-radar;
*** Range-finding (e.g. LIDAR);
*** Fixed-distance (e.g. inductive loops, laser barriers, light barriers, etc.);
*** Image based.
.. Average speed measurement between two remote positions:
*** Average speed meter (or section speed meter).
. Triggering and camera
.. Manual measurements (recommended with camera, possible to use system without
camera);
.. Automatic measurements (always with camera).
. Compensation for cosine error
.. Along-the-road speed meter (see <<scls-3-4-10>>);
.. Across-the-road speed meter (see <<scls-3-4-11>>).

NOTE: The same speed meter might be assigned to several categories (e.g. stationary
and moving mode of use implemented, mobile and fixed installations possible, etc.).
