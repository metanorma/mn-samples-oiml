== Withdrawal of a Utilizer or an Associate from the OIML-CS

=== {blank}

A Utilizer or an Associate may decide, for operational, business or commercial reasons, to withdraw from the OIML-CS. In the event that a Utilizer or an Associate wishes to withdraw from the OIML-CS a representative from the Utilizer or Associate shall inform the Executive Secretary.

=== {blank}

The Executive Secretary shall update the relevant information on the OIML-CS pages of the OIML website and shall inform the Management Committee.
