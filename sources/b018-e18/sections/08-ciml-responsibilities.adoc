== Responsibilities of the CIML within the OIML-CS

The CIML is responsible for

. appointing the Chairperson of the MC,

. appointing the Deputy Chairperson of the MC,

. appointing the Chairperson and members of the BoA,

. approving revisions to OIML B 18 Framework for the OIML Certification System (OIML-CS), and

. approving proposals of the MC concerning

.. moving a category of measuring instrument (and the applicable OIML Recommendation) from Scheme A back to Scheme B,
.. extending or reducing the two-year transition period for a category of measuring instrument when moving from Scheme B to Scheme A,
.. including a new category of measuring instrument in the OIML-CS, and
.. withdrawing previous editions of an OIML Recommendation in the OIML-CS (see <<operation>>).
