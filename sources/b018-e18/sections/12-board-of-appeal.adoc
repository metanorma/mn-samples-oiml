== Board of Appeal (BoA)

The BoA is independent of the MC.

[13.1] Composition

. a Chairperson appointed by the CIML; and

. four members appointed by the CIML.

[13.2] The tasks of the BoA are to

. manage appeals against decisions of the MC relating to

. participation in the OIML-CS, and
. Legal Metrology Experts and Management System Experts,

. recommend solutions to any other dispute referred to it with regard to the application of the rules of the OIML-CS.
