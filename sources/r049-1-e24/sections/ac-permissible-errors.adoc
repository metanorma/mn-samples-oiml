== Permissible errors in service and subsequent verification

The MPEs of a water meter while in service should be twice the MPEs given in
<<scls-4-2-2>> or <<scls-4-2-3>> according to the accuracy class of the meter.
Although subsequent verification is not covered in the scope of this part of
ISO 4064|OIML R 49, historically this has been found to be reasonable. Subsequent
verification should be applied in accordance with national regulations for legal
metrology.
