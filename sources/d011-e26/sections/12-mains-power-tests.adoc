== External wiring and mains power supply related performance tests

Methods for determining the degradation of performance of mains connected EUTs
during electrical mains power and external wiring conducted variations and disturbances.

=== DC mains variations (within network specification)

[[table-19]]
.DC mains voltage variation
[cols="<.<25a,<.<75a"]
|===
| Applicable standard
| <<IEC_60654_2_1979>>.

| Test method
| Applying low- and high-level DC mains power voltage.

| Applicability
| Applicable for measuring instruments which are temporarily or permanently
connected to a DC mains power network while in operation and generally only
applicable in an industrial environment.

| Object of the test
| Verification of compliance with the provisions in <<scls-5-1-1>> or <<scls-5-1-2>>
under conditions of DC mains power voltage changes between upper and lower limit.

| Test procedure in brief
| The test comprises exposure to the specified power supply condition for a
period sufficient for achieving temperature stability and subsequently performing
the required measurements.

| Test level index
| 1

| Test level
| The upper voltage limit is the DC level at which the EUT has been designed
to automatically detect high-level conditions.

The lower limit will be the DC level at which the EUT has been designed to automatically
detect low-level conditions.

The EUT shall comply with the specified maximum permissible errors at voltage
levels between the two levels.

Testing may be restricted to the exposure of the EUT to the upper and lower
voltage level, subsequently.
|===

[cols="<.<23a,^.^11a,^.^11a,^.^11a,^.^11a,^.^11a,^.^11a,^.^11a"]
|===
| Applicable standard
7+<.<| <<IEC_61000_4_5_2017>>.

| Test method
7+<.<| Introducing electrical surges on the mains power lines.

| Applicability
7+<.<| Applicable for electronic measuring instruments which are temporarily

| Object of the test
7+<.<| Verification of compliance with the provisions in <<scls-5-1-1>> or <<scls-5-1-2>>

| Test procedure in brief
7+<.<| A surge generator as defined in the referred standard shall be used.

and the minimum time interval between two successive pulses are defined in the
referred standard.
