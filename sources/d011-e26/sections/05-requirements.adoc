
== Requirements for measuring instruments with respect to their environment

Measuring instruments shall comply with the following requirements, notwithstanding
all other technical and metrological requirements of the applicable OIML Recommendation,
when installed and used in accordance with the manufacturers' specifications.

[[scls-5-1]]
=== General requirements

[[scls-5-1-1]]
==== {blank}

Measuring instruments shall be designed and manufactured such that their errors
do not exceed the maximum permissible errors under rated operating conditions.

[[scls-5-1-2]]
==== {blank}

Measuring instruments shall be designed and manufactured such that when they
are exposed to disturbances, either

. [[item-5-1-2-a]]significant faults do not occur, or
. [[item-5-1-2-b]]significant faults are detected and acted upon by means of
a checking facility.

NOTE: A fault equal to or smaller than the value fixed (fault limit) in the
applicable OIML Recommendation as defined in <<scls-3-11>> is allowed irrespective
of the value of the error of indication.

[[scls-5-1-3]]
==== {blank}

The provisions in <<scls-5-1-1>> and <<scls-5-1-2>> shall be durably met. Measuring
instruments shall be designed and manufactured such that either

. [[item-5-1-3-a]]significant durability errors do not occur, or
. [[item-5-1-3-b]]significant durability errors are detected and acted upon
by means of a durability protection facility.

==== {blank}

The type of a measuring instrument is presumed to comply with the provisions
in <<scls-5-1-1>>, <<scls-5-1-2>> and <<scls-5-1-3>> if it passes the examination
and tests specified in <<scls-6-2>>.

=== Application

==== {blank}

The provisions in <<item-5-1-2-a>> and <<item-5-1-2-b>> may be applied separately to

. each individual cause of significant fault, and/or
. each part of the measuring instrument.

==== {blank}

The choice of whether <<item-5-1-2-a>> or <<item-5-1-2-b>> is applied is left
to the manufacturer, unless the applicable OIML Recommendation specifies otherwise
in view of the intended use of the measuring instrument or the nature of measurement
(see <<note-4-3>>).

==== {blank}

The provisions in <<item-5-1-3-a>> and <<item-5-1-3-b>> may be applied separately
to each part of the measuring instrument (for example: analogue and digital parts).

==== {blank}

The choice of whether <<item-5-1-3-a>> or <<item-5-1-3-b>> is applied is left
to the manufacturer, unless the applicable OIML Recommendation specifies otherwise.

[[scls-5-3]]
=== Measuring instruments equipped with checking facilities

[[scls-5-3-1]]
==== {blank}

For each function of a measuring instrument the applicable OIML Recommendation
may specify

. the type of checking facility (P, I or N), as defined in <<scls-3-19>>,
. the checking frequency, if appropriate,
. the method of acting upon a significant fault.

[[scls-5-3-2]]
==== {blank}

The applicable OIML Recommendation may specify that it shall be possible to
determine the presence and correct functioning of these facilities.

==== {blank}

The requirements in <<scls-5-3-1>> and <<scls-5-3-2>> do not apply to measuring
instruments or parts of measuring instruments for which the manufacturer claims
compliance with the provisions in <<item-5-1-2-a>> and which are nevertheless
equipped with checking facilities.

[[scls-5-4]]
=== Measuring instruments equipped with durability protection facilities

[[scls-5-4-1]]
==== {blank}

The applicable OIML Recommendation may specify

. details concerning the operation of the durability protection facilities, and/or
. the method of acting upon the detection of significant durability errors.

[[scls-5-4-2]]
==== {blank}

The applicable OIML Recommendation may specify that it shall be possible to
determine the presence and correct operation of these facilities.

==== {blank}

The applicable OIML Recommendation may specify that requirements in <<scls-5-4-1>>
and <<scls-5-4-2>> do not apply to measuring instruments or parts of measuring
instruments for which the manufacturer claims compliance with the provision
in <<item-5-1-3-a>> and which are nevertheless equipped with durability protection
facilities.

[[scls-5-5]]
=== Requirements for battery powered instruments

==== Specification of batteries

Type(s) and capacity(ies) of the batteries allowed to be used in the applicable
measuring instruments shall be specified by the manufacturer. Instruments not
equipped with allowed batteries are not considered to be of the same type.

[[scls-5-5-2]]
==== Stand-alone batteries

Instruments powered by stand-alone batteries shall comply with the following
requirements:

. instruments provided with new and fully charged batteries shall comply with
the metrological requirements;
. [[item-5-5-2-b]]the manufacturer of the measuring instrument shall specify
the minimum battery voltage stem:[V_"min"] at which the instrument complies
with the metrological requirements;
. the instrument shall detect and react appropriately when the voltage is below
stem:[V_"min"]. The relevant OIML Technical Committee, Subcommittee or Project
Groups shall establish appropriate action for operation below stem:[V_"min"];
. the battery capacity and lifetime shall be in accordance with the application.
The applicable OIML Recommendation may prescribe a required minimum lifetime.

For such instruments, tests associated with the mains power variations and interferences
need not be performed.

The applicable OIML Recommendation shall specify a minimum set of provisions
to prevent the loss of stored data.

[[scls-5-5-3]]
==== Rechargeable auxiliary batteries

Instruments powered by rechargeable auxiliary batteries that are intended to
be (re)charged during the operation of the measuring instrument shall both

. comply with the requirements of <<scls-5-5-2>> with the mains power switched
off, and
. comply with the requirements for mains powered instruments with the mains
power switched on.

==== Back-up batteries

Instruments powered by the mains power and provided with a back-up battery for
data-storage only, shall comply with the requirements for mains powered instruments.

In the applicable OIML Recommendation a minimum period of time shall be stated
during which the applicable function of the instrument shall function properly
without replacing or recharging the batteries.

The provisions of <<item-5-5-2-b>> and <<scls-5-5-3>> do not apply for back-up
batteries.
