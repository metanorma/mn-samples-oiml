
== Mechanical performance tests

=== Vibration

In <<table-15>> and <<table-16>> two vibration tests (random and sinusoidal)
are described. In general, it should be avoided to require both tests in OIML
Recommendations.

Implementation of the random vibration test is preferred in OIML Recommendations.

The sinusoidal vibration test shall be applied only in those cases where the
measuring instrument is expected to be typically subjected to sinusoidal vibrations.

For both tests, selection guidance can be found in <<IEC_60068_3_8_2003>>.

4+<.<| Applicable for measuring instruments used in locations where predominantly

| Test procedure in brief
4+<.<| The test comprises exposure to vibration for a time sufficient for testing
the various functions of the EUT during the exposure.

The EUT shall be tested by exposing it to the specified acceleration level and
sweeping the vibration frequency in the specified frequency range, at
stem:[1 "octave/min"], for the specified number of sweep cycles per axis.

The EUT shall be tested in three, mutually perpendicular main axes while mounted
on a rigid fixture by its normal mounting means.

The EUT shall normally be mounted in such a way that the gravity vector points
in the same direction as it would in normal use. If the measurement principle
is such that the effect of the direction of the gravity vector can be considered
negligible, the EUT may be mounted in any position.

=== Mechanical shock

|===
| Applicable standard
3+<.<| <<IEC_60068_2_31_2008>>.

| Test method
3+<.<| Dropping the EUT onto a rigid surface after tilting.

| Applicability
3+<.<| Devices normally intended to remain fixed or stationary.

| Object of the test
3+<.<| Verification of compliance with the provisions in <<scls-5-1-1>> or <<scls-5-1-2>>
under conditions of mechanical shocks.

| Test procedure in brief
3+<.<| The EUT, standing in its normal position of use on a rigid surface, is
tilted along one bottom edge and is subsequently allowed to fall freely back
on to the test surface.

The height of fall is the distance between the opposite bottom edge and the
test surface. However, the angle between the bottom and the test surface shall
not exceed stem:[30 "unitsml(deg)"].
